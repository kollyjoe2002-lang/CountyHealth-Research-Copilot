## Deterministic APIs

Each API takes a Phase 3 ResolvedQuery plus mandatory `database_path=Path(...)`.
There is no default database path. Missing files raise instead of creating a
new database. APIs open DuckDB with read_only=True and use one consistent read
transaction. They are deliberately absent from the Research Assistant allowlist.

- `get_stratum_values`: validate the normalized envelope, resolve exact labels
  through existing small demographic catalogs, read requested IHME rows and
  RUCC metadata, and return every candidate county/year/stratum cell with its
  observation and geography status before exclusions. No scientific results
  are inferred for missing cells.
- `compare_strata`: compare two demographic groups within the same county;
  return complete pairs plus independent A/B availability and exclusions.
- `summarize_by_rurality`: summarize one fixed population in the selected RUCC
  groups with equal-county means and medians; return per-group and overall
  coverage. Optional rurality pair selection is still two disjoint summaries,
  never a paired county contrast or a composite outcome.

The immutable outputs include StratumValues, StratumDefinition,
StratumObservation, CountyComparison, ComparisonResult, PairedCoverage,
RuralityCoverage, RuralitySummary and RuralityResult. Detailed observations,
original query, marginal source bounds and source-specific evidence semantics
remain available alongside summaries for inspection.

## Analytical correctness and missingness

All reader SQL uses registry-owned relations/columns and bound data values.
The current county universe comes from analytics.vw_current_county_lookup,
with requested county/state scope applied first. Unmatched outcomes are kept
as diagnostic cells rather than lost through an inner join. RUCC is projected
onto the candidate county universe without dropping unclassified counties.

Only requested causes, observation years, measure=YLL (4), metric=Rate (3)
and exact demographic IDs are read. IDs are looked up by exact catalog label;
fixture IDs intentionally differ from legacy constants. Age 40-44 maps to the
catalog label 40 to 44; standardized 20+ maps to its separate explicit label.
The reader never replaces explicit filters with default sex/age/race IDs.

Before comparison or summary, duplicate burden keys fail explicitly. The
requested-key check covers FIPS/year/sex/age/race with cause/measure/metric
already fixed by the SQL predicates. Even duplicate null/suppressed rows are
not deduplicated. Duplicate county/RUCC keys and ambiguous requested catalogs
also fail. There is no MAX/AVG pivot or arbitrary row selection in reader SQL.

Observation statuses are independent of geography statuses:

- available: finite valid numeric estimate, including zero;
- absent_row: no row for that county and requested resolved combination;
- null_estimate: row exists but its estimate is null;
- unavailable_combination: a required catalog value cannot be resolved, or
  an explicit normalized source declaration marks the combination unavailable;
- source_declared_suppression: an explicit normalized source declaration says
  suppressed; values and bounds are withheld even if numbers are present.

A completely empty cause/year/combination with otherwise resolvable catalogs
remains a set of absent rows; absence is not promoted into a claim of globally
unsupported source coverage. Unresolvable-catalog diagnostics retain their
exact reason and do not establish real warehouse cross-product availability.

The raw schema currently has no source-status field. The optional adapter
column source_status is accepted only as VARCHAR with exact observed,
unavailable or suppressed tokens; a different schema/token fails. Its intended
contract is normalized verified source declarations, not inferred suppression.
Fixture declarations are synthetic and are not proof of real suppression
metadata. No source-status column means no suppression is inferred.

Signed demographic gap is A-B. Magnitude is abs(A-B). Relative gap preserves
legacy semantics: (A-B)/B*100, unavailable for B=0. Source uncertainty intervals
are retained separately for A and B. No uncertainty interval for the difference,
significance test, population-weighted national estimate or causal claim is
computed. Invalid/non-finite rates or marginal bounds fail explicitly.

Source semantics retain IHME uncertainty intervals, exact age adjustment,
selected observation years and the explicit population basis. Attribution and
unit scale remain unknown: numeric IDs and source filenames do not verify risk
attribution or rate scale. Caller-supplied attribution attestations cannot
replace the independent warehouse/import audit; the isolated reader currently
requires unknown attribution and does not emit stronger language.

## Coverage structure and denominators

Let C be the candidate current-county universe after explicit county/state
scope, M the counties with missing RUCC, O the known counties outside the
requested rurality selection, and E=C-M-O the eligible geography scope. For
non-rural analyses M=O=0. E is established before outcome matching. Missing
RUCC includes absent classification rows and null codes; invalid codes fail.

For paired comparisons let P be counties with both A and B available in E:

- candidate_counties=C; eligible_counties=E;
- pre_join_group_a_available and pre_join_group_b_available cover C;
- group_a_available and group_b_available cover E independently;
- complete_paired_counties=P;
- excluded_counties=C-P; outcome_excluded_counties=E-P;
- missing_rucc_counties=M; outside_geography_counties=O;
- paired_coverage_percent=100*P/E; universe_retention_percent=100*P/C.

For a fixed-population summary let V be available observations in E:

- observations_available=V; missing_outcome_observations=E-V;
- pre_join_observations_available records health availability across C;
- excluded_counties=C-V; coverage_percent=100*V/E;
- universe_retention_percent=100*V/C; M and O remain explicit.

Zero denominators return unavailable percentages (None), never zero-valued
estimates. Coverage is also reported per selected RUCC group and per year,
including empty groups/years. Unclassified counties cannot be assigned to a
known RUCC stratum, so their count is reported at the overall universe level.

Mutually exclusive primary exclusion reasons reconcile exactly to total
exclusions. Separate A/B status counts can overlap at county level. Every
observation retains both health and geography status, so missing RUCC does not
hide a simultaneous missing/null/suppressed health observation. The distinction
between eligible-scope coverage and original-universe retention prevents an
apparently complete matched subset from concealing geography join losses.

## Performance and SQL-design findings

There are no per-county database queries. One predicate-scoped burden read
serves all requested years and strata; small dimension catalog lookups are
cached within the call. RUCC/current-county lookups are scoped, and no
warehouse-wide demographic DISTINCT scan or materialized table is added.
One read transaction avoids catalog/outcome snapshot drift.

Rows are intentionally retained in a dense county/year/stratum matrix for
coverage auditing; memory grows with that requested matrix. Comparison and
summary operate only after uniqueness validation. The fixture gate found no
structural SQL failures, but tiny-fixture execution time is not a warehouse
benchmark. Full-scale scan cost, memory, query plans and latency on the large
burden table remain unmeasured. Exact source FIPS/catalog consistency and
join coverage also remain real-data gates, with no automatic geography alias
or demographic-default substitution to hide discrepancies.
