## Release Summary

EpiCounty v1.2.0 expands the CountyHealth Research Copilot from its original IHME-based analytical system into a validated multi-source county public-health research platform integrating CDC PLACES, IHME disease burden estimates, and USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes.

The release preserves deterministic analytical execution while extending semantic routing, demographic disparity analysis, rurality comparison, cross-source trend analysis, evidence provenance, and guarded AI interpretation.

## Cross-Source Analytical Capabilities

V1.2 adds deterministic PLACES-IHME analytical functions for:

- county context
- county trends
- rurality comparisons
- positive-deviance exploration
- model diagnostics
- race disparity context and ranking
- sex disparity context and ranking
- age disparity context and ranking
- generic demographic disparity analysis
- demographic disparity trend analysis

Cross-source evidence systems remain analytically distinct. PLACES prevalence and IHME YLL burden are not combined into a synthetic metric.

## Concept Compatibility Guardrails

Approved PLACES-IHME mappings are intentionally conservative:

- `DIABETES` ? IHME cause 976, Diabetes mellitus type 2
- `STROKE` ? IHME cause 494, Stroke
- `CASTHMA` ? IHME cause 515, Asthma

Unsupported pairings fail closed.

In particular, CDC PLACES `CANCER` is not treated as breast cancer and is never automatically mapped to IHME breast cancer.

## Semantic and AI Layer

V1.2 includes:

- validated OpenAI semantic parsing
- deterministic semantic request policy
- canonical research-assistant orchestration
- structured research outcomes
- evidence-grounded AI interpretation
- lexical numeric entailment safeguards
- model entailment validation
- fail-closed handling for unsupported forecasting, causality, and hypothesis-testing requests
- clarification handling for ambiguous counties and underspecified questions

## Evidence-Language Guardrails

The release enforces distinctions between:

- prevalence and disease burden
- descriptive association and causation
- modeled estimates and direct survey observations
- PLACES confidence intervals and IHME uncertainty intervals
- county context and demographic disparity evidence

Cross-source interpretations are explicitly descriptive and do not imply causal relationships.

## Reporting and Provenance

V1.2 adds source-aware report provenance for analytical outputs and exports, including identification of the underlying evidence system used in each analysis.
