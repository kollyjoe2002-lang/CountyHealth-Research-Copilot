# CountyHealth Research Copilot

## Version

0.1 (Foundational Design)

---

# Vision

To build an AI-assisted epidemiology platform that enables researchers to explore county-level disease burden, health disparities, and population health trends using natural language and reproducible analytics.

---

# Mission

Reduce the time between asking a public health research question and obtaining scientifically valid, reproducible, and interpretable analyses.

---

# Problem Statement

Public health datasets are large, fragmented, and technically difficult to analyze.

Researchers often spend days or weeks cleaning data and writing statistical code before answering relatively simple epidemiologic questions.

CountyHealth Research Copilot will reduce this effort by integrating structured public health datasets with AI-assisted analytics.

---

# Initial Target Users

* Epidemiologists
* Public Health Researchers
* MPH Students
* PhD Students
* Faculty Researchers
* State and Local Health Departments

---

# Primary Data Sources (Phase 1)

* IHME High BMI Prevalence and Years of Life Lost by County, Race, Ethnicity and Cause (2000–2019)

---

# Future Data Sources

* CDC PLACES
* CDC WONDER
* American Community Survey
* U.S. Census Population Estimates
* NHANES
* BRFSS
* County Health Rankings
* USDA Food Environment Atlas

---

# Scientific Scope

The platform focuses on:

* Disease burden
* Health disparities
* Geographic variation
* Time trends
* Obesity-attributable disease
* Chronic disease epidemiology
* Population health surveillance

---

# AI Scope

Artificial intelligence will assist—not replace—epidemiologic analysis.

Primary AI functions include:

* Natural language querying
* Automatic statistical interpretation
* Research hypothesis generation
* Literature-assisted explanation
* Report generation

---

# Core Principles

1. Scientific validity comes before AI convenience.
2. Every result must be reproducible.
3. Data provenance must always be preserved.
4. AI interpretations should be transparent and evidence-based.
5. The platform should support both research and education.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Develop CountyHealth Research Copilot into a modular public health intelligence platform capable of integrating multiple national datasets and supporting AI-assisted epidemiologic research across chronic diseases, health disparities, and population health.
